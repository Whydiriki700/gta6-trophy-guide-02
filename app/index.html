Roadmap to the platinum

Six stages, in order. The story first, then strangers and contacts, then activities, heists, collectibles, and finally money and cleanup. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay — and keep cheats off for the whole run, since cheats disable trophies.

    Missable watchlist

    Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable by the guide sites: Positive Mental Attitude — Lucia's balcony meditation spot is offered in Chapter 1 and disappears after that chapter. Meditate there before the chapter's final mission. Everything else is reported unmissable, but the chapter-start manual saves protect you anyway.
